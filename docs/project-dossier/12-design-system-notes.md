# Design System Notes

## Business rationale

The design system is tuned for analyst productivity: fast scanability, clear hierarchy, and immediate visibility into confidence and risk cues.

## What this means in practice

- Information density is intentional, not decorative.
- Section ordering supports progression from summary to evidence.
- Visual emphasis is used to highlight confidence, gaps, and action-relevant findings.
